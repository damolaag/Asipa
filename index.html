His Life

A life of integrity, generosity and love

It is with profound sadness but hopeful hearts that we remember our beloved father, husband, brother, uncle, in-law and friend, who entered eternal rest on Thursday, July 16, 2026.

He was a man of integrity, kindness, strength, and love for his family. His wisdom, generosity and gentle spirit touched the lives of many, and he will be deeply missed by all who knew him.
